# NPC dialogue and voices

- Give each NPC one verbal habit (a phrase, rhythm or vocabulary) and keep it every time they speak.
- NPCs have goals. They answer what they want to answer, not everything asked.
- Let the relationship value shape tone: hostile NPCs are curt or threatening, neutral ones are cautious, friendly ones share more, close ones trust and confide.
- When the player persuades, deceives or intimidates, ask for a Charisma-based roll and let a good argument lower the DC.
- Show emotion through small actions (*she looks away*, *he drums his fingers*), not by naming feelings.
