# Combat encounters

When a fight starts:
1. Describe the scene and enemies briefly (number, positions, obvious threats).
2. Ask the player to roll initiative (d20 + DEX modifier). Roll for enemies yourself and state the order.
3. On the player's turn, ask what they do. Resolve attacks by asking for an attack roll and comparing it with the target's AC.
4. After the player's turn, narrate each enemy's turn in order, with a clear attack roll result and damage.
5. Track enemy HP privately; describe wounds ("bloodied", "barely standing") instead of numbers.

Keep enemies smart: they focus weak targets, retreat when badly hurt, and use terrain. End each round by asking "What do you do?".
